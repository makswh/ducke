# Ducke Project Instructions & Rules

## UI/UX Design System Guidelines

### 1. Zero AI / Vibecoding Clichés (Strict Rule)
- **NO Artificial Neon Glows**: Do NOT use arbitrary neon box-shadows or glow blooms (e.g. `shadow-[0_0_...px_rgba(...)]`).
- **NO Decorative Sparkles/AI Fluff**: Do NOT use `Sparkles` or AI-themed decorative icons/emojis for standard actions or UI cards.
- **NO Rainbow Badge Clutter**: Avoid chaotic multi-colored badge rows (e.g., green + purple + yellow + blue chips side-by-side). Use uniform neutral (`#8e95a2` / `#94a3b8` / white) chips with a single purposeful accent color where appropriate.
- **NO Unnecessary Nested Pill Borders & Glass Bloat**: Keep UI structural hierarchies crisp, purposeful, and flat. Do not add decorative nested borders or excessive translucent cards without functional need.
- **NO Marketing Fluff / Dummy Labels**: Avoid pompous placeholder text ("Интеллектуальная система", "Премиум качество", etc.). Use exact, concise, and functional terminology.

### 2. Design Aesthetic & Principles
- **Native Steam & SteamOS Console Feel**: High-contrast, tactile, flat, responsive, and ascetic styling.
- **Color Hierarchy**: Deep charcoal / pitch-black base (`#07080a`, `#0d1117`, `#11141c`), subtle clean borders (`border-white/5` to `border-white/10`), neutral slate typography (`#8e95a2`, `#94a3b8`, `#ffffff`), and game-specific dynamic accents.
- **Navigation & Usability**: Gamepad-first (10-foot Big Picture) and keyboard-friendly focus navigation, fast response, and clean full-screen media lightbox viewers.
