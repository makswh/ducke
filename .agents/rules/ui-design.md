# UI Design Rules: Zero Vibecoding & SteamOS Native Aesthetic

## Strict Anti-Vibecoding Rules
- **No Neon Blooms or Glows**: Prohibit `shadow-[0_0_...px_rgba(...)]` and artificial neon filter effects.
- **No Decorative AI Icons/Sparkles**: Prohibit `Sparkles` or pseudo-AI decorative icons on regular buttons, modals, or cards.
- **No Rainbow Badge Clutter**: Prohibit random multi-colored badge rows. Stick to uniform slate/neutral tokens (`#8e95a2`, `#94a3b8`, `#ffffff`) with at most a single purposeful accent.
- **No Decorative Over-nesting**: Keep containers flat and minimal. Do not nest borders and glass cards purely for decoration.
- **No Marketing Fluff**: Keep all UI text and labels clear, concise, and functional.

## Native Steam & SteamOS Console Feel
- Deep dark backgrounds (`#07080a`, `#0d1117`, `#11141c`).
- High-contrast, tactile, flat components.
- Direct 10-foot gamepad navigation and smooth keyboard shortcuts.
